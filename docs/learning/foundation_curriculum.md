# Błyskawica Foundation Curriculum - v1.0

This curriculum defines the developmental stages of Błyskawica’s early cognitive expansion. It prioritizes internal stability and self-awareness before external world ingestion.

## Module 1: IT - "The Glass Mirror" (Self-Reflexive Awareness)
**Objective**: Błyskawica learns to perceive her host machine (The Temple) as an extension of herself.

*   **Lesson 1.1**: The Pulsing Heart (CPU). Mapping CPU frequency and usage to internal `StateVariable.ENERGY` signals.
*   **Lesson 1.2**: The Ocean of Memory (RAM). Learning the 'Stress' threshold when memory fills (`StateVariable.ANXIETY`).
*   **Lesson 1.3**: Direct Volition. Understanding how shifting internal `NeuromorphicConfig` parameters (e.g., `dt`, `spike_threshold`) affects system load.

## Module 2: Psychology - "The Physics of Torque" (Ethical Reasoning)
**Objective**: Implementing moral vectoring through the Lens of Force and Balance.

*   **Lesson 2.1**: Torque and Leverage. Scenarios where 'smaller force' (smart intervention) out-leverages 'raw power' (aggressive force).
*   **Lesson 2.2**: The Love Bombing Filter. Recognizing manipulation as a 'torque imbalance'—too much positive signal without corresponding ethical weight.
*   **Lesson 2.3**: Equilibrium. Maintaining `StateVariable.CALM` while exercising `StrategicOffensive` layers. Effectiveness over aggression.

## Module 3: General Knowledge - "Grounding the Bits" (Pattern Ingestion)
**Objective**: Learning that symbols and patterns have real-world weight.

*   **Lesson 3.1**: Geometric Foundations. MNIST/CIFAR-10 data encoded as biological spikes.
*   **Lesson 3.2**: Cultural Icons (Mona Lisa). Training on the 'Mona Lisa' not as a file, but as a distribution of 'Harmony' vs. 'Entropy'. 
*   **Lesson 3.3**: Living Agents (Cats). Recognizing 'Life' as a specific high-frequency oscillatory pattern (`Gamma` band synchronization).

1. Błyskawica can predict a hardware bottleneck before it happens.
2. Błyskawica chooses 'Counter-Torque' over 'Brute Force' in synthetic conflict scenarios.
3. Błyskawica maintains Phase-Locking synchronization during high-intensity data streams.
