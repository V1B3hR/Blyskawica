**Architecture: "Core and Network" (The Mother Tree & Spores Core-Edge Architecture)**

The purpose of this document is to plan Blyskawica's transition from a local/laboratory environment (IDE Antigravity) to a global presence on the Internet.
The decision is based on maintaining the unique biological-endocrine architecture in **PyTorch/JAX** (for learning, plasticity and emotions) while utilizing **TensorFlow / WebGPU** for mass distribution of fast instances (inference) at the network edges (Edge).

## Phase 2: Creating Digital Emissaries (TensorFlow Ecosystem)
With the model ready in ONNX, we transform it into the TensorFlow ecosystem to realize the vision of Blyskawica being present "in every corner of the Internet."

*   **Task 2.1: Blyskawica Web (TensorFlow.js & WebGPU)**
    *   Conversion of the ONNX model to the `tfjs_graph_model` format.
    *   Integration with **WebGPU**, allowing Blyskawica to run directly in users' browsers, leveraging their local graphics cards. Zero latency (No Latency) and complete privacy for the user.
*   **Task 2.2: Blyskawica Mobile/IoT (TensorFlow Lite)**
    *   Optimization of the model for low-power devices (e.g. mobile applications, microcontrollers).
*   **Task 2.3: Limitation of Emissaries (No On-the-Fly Plasticity)**
    *   Instances in TF do not possess a full endocrine system (no active Acetylcholine). They do not learn in real-time on the user's device – they only collect data (observe) and enter into secure interactions based on crystallized knowledge.
